What We Are

SendCrypto.io is a rate comparison and information platform for people converting cryptocurrency to fiat currency. We aggregate live rate data from licensed, regulated providers across more than 40 remittance corridors worldwide.

We are not a money transmitter. We do not hold, transfer, or settle funds. Every conversion happens directly on the platform you choose — a licensed, regulated provider we independently evaluate and rank.

Our revenue comes from affiliate commissions when users click through and complete transactions on partner platforms, and from display advertising. This is fully disclosed in our affiliate policy.

What We Are Not

We are not a bank. We are not a licensed money services business. We do not provide financial advice. Rate data on this platform is for comparison purposes only and is not a guarantee of pricing.

We are not paid by providers to rank them higher. Our algorithm ranks based on net rate received by the sender — the amount that hits the recipient's account after all fees. Best deal for the user ranks first.

We have no proprietary relationship with any single provider that influences our organic rankings. Sponsored placement is disclosed with a visible "Sponsored" label and does not alter the organic comparison.

How We Make Money

Affiliate Commissions

When you click through to a provider and complete a transaction, we may earn a referral fee from that provider. This does not increase your cost — providers pay us from their margin.

Display Advertising

We run display advertising from crypto and fintech advertisers. Advertisers cannot purchase placement in our comparison rankings. Ads are visually separated from organic results.

Sponsored Placements

Providers may pay for featured placement outside the organic ranking table. All sponsored placements carry a visible "Sponsored" label. They do not alter organic comparison results.

Our Data Sources

Live API Integration

Where providers offer a public or partner API, we pull live rate data on a 15-minute refresh cycle powered by CoinGecko and provider endpoints cached in our Supabase database.

Manual Verification

Providers without public APIs are monitored manually and updated at least daily. These entries are flagged with a "Manually Updated" indicator in the comparison table.

Provider Vetting

Before a provider enters our index, we verify their licensing status, check for regulatory actions in their operating markets, and confirm their fee structure. We re-verify quarterly.
